What the rules require me to tell you

  • The most one person can give to my campaign is $1,200.
  • One person can give a total of $5,000 across all candidates running for Perth council.
  • Only individuals who live in Ontario can contribute. Corporations, unions and groups cannot.
  • Anything over $25 has to be traceable, so no cash above that amount.
  • Contributions over $100 are recorded and appear in the campaign financial statement, which is a public document.
  • This is not a charitable donation. There is no tax receipt. You'll get a receipt for your own records.

These rules come from Ontario's Municipal Elections Act. If anything here is unclear, email me and I'll walk you through it.
